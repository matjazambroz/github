FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which languages do you work with?

We cover a wide range of language pairs for translation and interpretation. Tell us your source and target languages and we'll confirm coverage and match you with the right native-speaking linguists.

How is AI actually used in my translation?

AI-assisted tools speed up first drafts, reuse translation memory, and flag terminology or consistency issues early. Every translation is still reviewed and finalized by a native-speaking human linguist before delivery — AI never has the final word.

How fast can you turn a project around?

It depends on length, language pair, and complexity. Share your content and deadline through the contact form and we'll confirm a realistic timeline before any work starts.

Can you match our existing terminology and style guide?

Yes. We build a glossary and style guide from your existing materials and preferences, then apply them consistently across every translator and project — see our terminology & style management service for details.

Do you offer interpretation for live events?

Yes, both on-site and remote (video or phone), in simultaneous or consecutive mode. Share your event date, format, and language pairs and we'll confirm interpreter availability.

How do I get a quote?

Use the contact form with your languages, content, and deadline, or email us directly at hello@amidas.si. We'll follow up with a scope and a quote.

Ready to communicate without barriers?

Tell us about your project — languages, content, and deadline — and we'll get back to you with a plan and a quote.
