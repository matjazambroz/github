Services / AI-Assisted Translation & QA

AI-assisted translation & QA, with a human always in the loop.

Translation memory and AI tools speed up first drafts and catch inconsistencies early — but every translation is reviewed and approved by a native-speaking linguist before it reaches you.

AI tools draft a first pass quickly by reusing your translation memory and applying your approved terminology, which shortens turnaround on large or recurring projects. Automated checks flag inconsistent terms, missed placeholders, and formatting issues before a linguist ever looks at the text.

That draft is only a starting point. A native-speaking linguist reviews and edits every segment for meaning, tone, and context — the parts AI still gets wrong — so the final translation reads naturally and holds up under scrutiny.
