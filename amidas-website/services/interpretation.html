Services / Interpretation

Interpretation for meetings, conferences, and live events.

On-site and remote interpreters help you communicate confidently in real time, in the language pairs and format your event needs.

Simultaneous interpretation keeps a live event moving in real time, ideal for conferences, webinars, and multi-language panels. Consecutive interpretation works better for smaller meetings, negotiations, and site visits, where the interpreter speaks after each part of the conversation.

Interpreters can join on-site or remotely over video or phone, and we match each assignment to an interpreter experienced in your subject area — legal, technical, medical, or business — so nothing gets lost in translation when it matters most.
