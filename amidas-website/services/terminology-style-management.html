Services / Terminology & Style Management

Terminology & style management that keeps every project consistent.

Shared glossaries and style guides make sure your brand voice and technical terms stay consistent — across languages, projects, and translators.

We build your glossary from the content you already have — past translations, product documentation, and any preferred terms you provide — then confirm it with you before it's applied to new work. A style guide captures tone, formatting conventions, and terms to avoid, so every translator works from the same playbook.

Both are living documents. As your product, market, or terminology evolves, we update the glossary and style guide and roll the changes into future projects, so consistency doesn't decay over time or across translators.

  • ✓ Style guide covering tone, formatting, and terminology
  • ✓ Applied consistently across every translator and project
  • ✓ Reviewed and updated as your business evolves
